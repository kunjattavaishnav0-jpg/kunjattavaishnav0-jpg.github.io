Vaishnav R (Vaishnav Kunjatta)

Student Leader, Social Activist, and Dubbing Artist from Karuvatta, Alappuzha, Kerala.

Key Roles:

  • President, Karuvatta Grama Panchayat Kudumbashree Balapanchayat
  • Minister for Social Welfare, Women & Child Development, Alappuzha District Bala Parliament
  • Secretary, Karuvatta 1st Ward 'Unarvu' Balasabha
  • Executive Committee Member, Kudumbashree Balasabha 'Operation Toofan'
  • Active Member of Jawahar Bal Manch (Indian National Congress / INC) & KSU
